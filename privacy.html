Privacy Policy

Review draft — the platform operator must verify and approve this document before public launch.

Information we handle

TopScore may process information you provide, including your name, email address, phone number, examination year, selected subjects, profile details, study activity, quiz and examination results, points, referral records and withdrawal details where applicable.

Why information is used

Information is used to create and secure accounts, deliver courses and examinations, calculate progress and rewards, operate subscriptions and referrals, provide support, prevent fraud and improve the service.

Service providers

The application is configured to use Firebase services for authentication, database and backend functions. Paystack integration is present for payment processing and may handle payment information when enabled. Their processing is also governed by their own privacy terms.

Sharing

TopScore should not sell students' personal information. Limited information may be processed by infrastructure and payment providers, disclosed where legally required, or displayed in leaderboards according to the platform's settings. Do not publish bank account details or private contact information on public pages.

Security and retention

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards should be used. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Records may be retained while needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes and prevent fraud.

Your choices

Contact TopScore through Help & Support to request access, correction or deletion of account information, subject to applicable legal and operational retention requirements.

Children and student accounts

Students should provide accurate information and, where required, obtain parent or guardian guidance before using paid features.

Before public launch

This is a starter policy draft, not legal advice. The platform operator must confirm actual data flows, retention periods, lawful bases, contact details, user-rights procedures and applicable Nigerian data-protection requirements with a qualified adviser before launch.
